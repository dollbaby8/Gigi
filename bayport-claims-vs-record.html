Garcia v. Bayport Laboratories, LLC  •  Public-Record Briefing

What Bayport Laboratories Said — vs. — What the Record Shows

Four key claims, checked against federal court filings, U.S. Department of Labor action, and public records.

1  “Stolen trade secrets — millions in damages”

The Claim

Bayport and CEO Yaumara Camacho counterclaimed for trade-secret misappropriation and sought sweeping emergency injunctions against the Garcias.

The Record

On Oct. 8, 2025, Judge Keith P. Ellison denied Bayport's TRO and preliminary-injunction requests except narrow NDA enforcement — and Bayport was left moving for reconsideration (Dkt. 44). Plaintiffs state no evidence of misappropriation has been produced.

Minute Entry, Oct. 8, 2025; Dkt. 6, 21, 44 — No. 4:25-cv-03676 (S.D. Tex.)

2  “The safety complaints were fabricated”

The Claim

Bayport dismissed reports of product contamination and sanitation failures as the invention of a disgruntled ex-employee.

The Record

Per plaintiffs' filings, the U.S. Department of Labor (OSHA) issued formal Section 11(c) whistleblower protection to Milagros Garcia after she reported those very conditions.

OSHA §11(c) matter as alleged in Pls.' filings; press record (OpenPR, Aug. 2025)

3  “There was no retaliation”

The Claim

Bayport maintains its lawsuits and terminations had nothing to do with the federal safety complaint.

The Record

Garcia was employed Feb. 10 – June 8, 2025 — fired after reporting — then sued, with Bayport demanding retraction of her federal OSHA complaint, a demand plaintiffs note federal law itself prohibits.

As alleged in Pls.' First Am. Compl. (Dkt. 20) and public coverage; contested by Defendants

4  The spotless-image PR campaign

The Claim

Paid placements — a “Leadership Spotlight” and “the Texas manufacturer behind top beauty brands” — present an unblemished record.

The Record

The placements omit the pending federal case, the OSHA whistleblower matter, and the founders' documented litigation history — including two SBA PPP loans totaling $751,686 now under scrutiny in plaintiffs' fraud allegations (unadjudicated).

Medium (@Bayportlabs); News By Wire “Image Fix” report; SBA PPP records (FederalPay.org)
