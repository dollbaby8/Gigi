Case No. 2023-CV-00417
In the Matter of Riverside Holdings LLC v. Crestline Partners

THIS MATTER having come before the Court on the plaintiff's application, and
the Court having considered the moving papers, the Court hereby GRANTS the
plaintiff's application for a temporary restraining order (TRO) against the
defendant.

IT IS HEREBY ORDERED that the defendant is restrained and enjoined from
transferring, encumbering, or dissipating the disputed joint venture assets
pending further order of this Court.

This temporary restraining order shall remain in effect until the hearing on
the plaintiff's motion for a preliminary injunction.

SO ORDERED this 1st day of February, 2023.
