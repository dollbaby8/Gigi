Case No. 2023-CV-00417
In the Matter of Riverside Holdings LLC v. Crestline Partners

THIS MATTER having come before the Court for hearing, and the Court having
reviewed the full record and the parties' submissions, the Court hereby
DISSOLVES the temporary restraining order (TRO) previously entered on
February 1, 2023.

The Court finds that the plaintiff has not demonstrated a likelihood of success
on the merits and has shown no basis for continued injunctive relief. The
restraint previously imposed on the defendant is lifted in its entirety.

The defendant is no longer enjoined from transacting in the disputed assets.

SO ORDERED this 10th day of April, 2023.
